01 — About the Community

Nature, woven in

Sobha Sanctuary is a future-ready master community by Sobha Realty, shaped around nature, balance and daily ease.

Residences, retail, wellness and everyday services sit within a walkable, green and well-connected masterplan. Forest walks, botanical gardens and a central park of around 800,000 sq.ft. flow through every neighbourhood, while a beach lagoon, community mall and sports district bring life to its heart.
