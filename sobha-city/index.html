01 — About the Community

Live. Connect. Restore.

Marking Sobha Realty’s arrival in Abu Dhabi, Sobha City brings nature, water and architecture together in one calm, connected community.

Refined apartments, garden villas and expansive estate homes sit within a landscape of parks, forest trails and a 2 km waterfront promenade. A community mall, yacht marina, clubhouse, international school, clinic and mosque mean every essential is close to home — and Yas Island is about 12 minutes away.

FAQ

Sobha City FAQ

What is Sobha City Abu Dhabi?

Sobha Realty’s first master community in Abu Dhabi, with three neighbourhoods: The Orchard (4, 5 and 6 bedroom estate villas), The Terraces (3 and 4 bedroom garden villas with a Majlis) and River Cove Residences (1 to 4 bedroom apartments).

When will Sobha City be completed?

The anticipated completion date (ACD) for all three neighbourhoods is December 2029.

What are the home sizes at Sobha City?

Estate villas at The Orchard are about 4,309 – 6,383 sq.ft., garden villas at The Terraces about 2,578 – 4,524 sq.ft., and River Cove apartments about 565 – 3,429 sq.ft.

Where is Sobha City located?

In Abu Dhabi, about 12 minutes from Yas Mall, 15 minutes from Ferrari World and 18 minutes from Zayed International Airport.
