FAQ

Questions buyers often ask

Which Sobha Realty projects can I buy through Espousal?

Espousal presents Sobha Central on Sheikh Zayed Road (apartments from AED 1.78 Million), Sobha Sanctuary (The Woods apartments from AED 999K, plus The Greens and The Grove villas), Sobha Elwood (4, 5 and 6 bedroom villas from AED 7.93 Million) and Sobha City in Abu Dhabi (villas and apartments).

Can Indians buy property in Dubai?

Yes. Foreign nationals, including Indian residents, can buy freehold property in Dubai’s designated areas. Payments from India are usually sent under the RBI’s Liberalised Remittance Scheme (currently USD 250,000 per person per financial year) — please confirm the current rules with your bank or chartered accountant. A payment plan spreads the payments over the construction period. Read our full guide.

What payment plans does Sobha Realty offer?

Plans differ by project: Sobha Central offers 40% during construction and 60% on completion; The Woods at Sobha Sanctuary and Sobha Elwood offer 60% during construction and 40% on completion. Plans can change, so ask our advisor for the current schedule.

Do I need to travel to Dubai to buy?

No. Our team can share prices, floor plans and availability on a call or video meeting, and arrange a site visit whenever you are in the UAE.

No. Espousal is an independent company and an Authorised Channel Partner of Sobha Realty. Sobha Realty is the developer of all the projects on this website.
