Which project suits you?

For a city address: Sobha Central

Six towers on Sheikh Zayed Road at the Bluewaters & JLT interchange, minutes from Dubai Marina, JBR and Palm Jumeirah. A good fit if you want a well-connected apartment with an easy 40/60 payment plan.

For nature and a lower entry price: Sobha Sanctuary

A green master community where about half the land is parks, with a beach lagoon and a 9 km wellness loop. The Woods apartments start from AED 999K — the lowest entry price on this website — and the community also offers garden and estate villas.

For a large private villa: Sobha Elwood

Standalone 4, 5 and 6 bedroom villas, each with a private pool, set among seven parks inspired by the world’s great forests. Sizes run from about 4,958 to 7,193 sq.ft.

For Abu Dhabi: Sobha City

Sobha Realty’s first Abu Dhabi community, with a yacht marina, a Greg Norman-designed Par 3 golf course and three neighbourhoods: The Orchard estate villas, The Terraces garden villas and River Cove Residences apartments.

Why buyers choose Sobha Realty

  • In-house quality: Sobha Realty designs, engineers and builds its own projects, with its own manufacturing for interiors and finishes.
  • Payment plans that spread the cost over the construction period.
  • Premium finishes such as Bosch or Siemens appliances and Duravit sanitaryware (or equivalent), depending on the project.
