01 — About the Project

Where it
all connects

Sobha Central is a landmark residential community rising on Sheikh Zayed Road, Dubai’s most iconic corridor.

This architectural giant unites six sleek and elegant towers around lush, elevated parks, a tech-powered retail mall, a dedicated office space and curated lifestyle amenities. Set along Sheikh Zayed Road, Sobha Central basks in breathtaking views of the sparkling Arabian Sea, the prestigious Jumeirah Lake Towers (JLT) and the bustling Dubai Marina — redefining the concept of integrated living, working and leisure.
