Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This website (espousalsobha.com/sobhacentral) is operated by Espousal, which markets Sobha Central by Sobha Realty. This policy explains what information we collect when you use the website, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

When you fill an enquiry form, request floor plans, download the brochure or ask for a site visit, we collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number and city
  • What you asked about (for example, a floor plan, the payment plan or a specific unit type)
  • The page you were on, the time of your enquiry, and how you found us (for example, an ad campaign name)

We also collect basic usage information through cookies and similar tools, described in section 4.

2. How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry by phone, WhatsApp or email
  • To send you the brochure, floor plans, prices and payment plan you asked for
  • To arrange site visits or meetings, if you request them
  • To understand which of our ads and pages are useful, and improve them

We do not sell your information. We will not send you marketing messages unrelated to your enquiry.

3. Who we share it with

Your details are used by the Espousal sales team. If you decide to book a unit or visit the Sobha sales office, we will share the details needed for that with Sobha Realty, with your agreement. We also use these service providers to run the website:

  • Google (Google Sheets) to store enquiries securely
  • FormSubmit to deliver enquiry emails to our team
  • Meta (Facebook / Instagram) to measure our ads, see section 4
  • GitHub Pages to host the website, and YouTube to show the virtual tour video

4. Cookies and the Meta Pixel

This website uses the Meta Pixel. It tells Meta when someone views the page, opens a floor plan, or submits an enquiry, so that we can measure our ads and show them to people likely to be interested. Meta may use cookies on your device for this. You can control ad preferences in your Facebook or Instagram settings, and you can block or delete cookies in your browser at any time.

The website also stores a small note in your browser after you fill a form, so floor plans stay unlocked during that visit.

5. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to help you with your purchase, and for up to 24 months after our last contact with you, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

6. Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Email masroorshad@gmail.com or WhatsApp +91 79807 02399, and we will respond within 30 days.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, and access is limited to people who need it to help you. No website or online service is completely secure, so please do not send sensitive documents (such as passport or bank details) through the website forms.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
