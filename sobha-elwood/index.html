01 — About the Community

An urban living retreat

Sobha Elwood is an exclusive villa community on Dubai–Al Ain Road, set among more than 10,000 trees and landscapes inspired by the world’s great forests.

It brings the calm of nature together with easy connections to the rest of Dubai. The masterplan is built around a central green spine, with direct access from every plot to open green areas, a clubhouse, community shopping centre, school, clinic, nursery and mosque on site, and a future-ready design with micromobility and EV charging.

FAQ

Sobha Elwood FAQ

What is the starting price of Sobha Elwood villas?

Villas start from AED 7.93 Million (about ₹18 crore*), as per Sobha Realty’s published material. Prices vary by villa type and plot.

What is the payment plan for Sobha Elwood?

60% during construction and 40% on completion. Ask our advisor for the full schedule.

What villa sizes are available?

4 bedroom villas are about 4,958 – 5,007 sq.ft., 5 bedroom villas about 5,820 sq.ft. and 6 bedroom villas about 7,193 sq.ft. (saleable area). Every villa is standalone with a private pool.

Where is Sobha Elwood located?

On Dubai–Al Ain Road — about 5 minutes from Dubai Rugby Sevens, 8 minutes from Dubai Outlet Mall, 20 minutes from Downtown Dubai and 25 minutes from Dubai International Airport.

What are the seven parks at Sobha Elwood?

Seven parks inspired by famous forests: Daintree, Baobab, Bristlecone, Białowieża, Amazon, Yakushima and Taiga — with more than 10,000 trees across the community.

Can Indians buy property in Dubai?

Yes. Foreign nationals, including Indian residents, can buy freehold property in Dubai’s designated areas. Payments from India are usually sent under the RBI’s Liberalised Remittance Scheme (currently USD 250,000 per person per financial year) — please confirm the current rules with your bank or chartered accountant. A payment plan spreads the payments over the construction period.

*Indian rupee figures are approximate — please check the current exchange rate. Prices and plans are subject to change.
