01 — About the Tower

Luxury, in every direction

Set between the twin interchanges on Ras Al Khor Road, The Element brings together the Downtown skyline, the calm of Dubai Creek and the wildlife sanctuary’s mangroves and flamingos.

This standalone tower at Sobha One has a clean, modern design with large windows and balconies in every home. Residents enjoy amenities on the podium and the rooftop, and the wider Sobha One community — an 18-hole pitch & putt golf course, a lagoon walk, a waterfront F&B esplanade, a polyclinic, nursery and mosque.

FAQ

The Element FAQ

Where is The Element at Sobha One?

On Ras Al Khor Road in Dubai, inside the Sobha One community, between twin interchanges. It looks over the Ras Al Khor Wildlife Sanctuary, Dubai Creek and the Downtown Dubai skyline.

What types of homes are at The Element?

1, 2, 3 and 4 bedroom apartments in a standalone tower. 1 and 2 bedroom homes are on levels 10 – 33; 3 and 4 bedroom homes, with a maid’s room, are on levels 37 – 60.

What are the apartment sizes?

Total saleable areas (including balcony) are about 737 – 754 sq.ft. for 1 bedroom, 1,184 – 1,516 sq.ft. for 2 bedroom, 1,853 – 1,958 sq.ft. for 3 bedroom and about 2,442 sq.ft. for the 4 bedroom.

What amenities does The Element offer?

A rooftop resort-style pool with over 270° panoramic views, a padel court, outdoor and indoor gyms, pilates and yoga studio, cinema room, co-working lounge, kids’ club and more — plus Sobha One’s 18-hole pitch & putt golf course and a 350 m+ F&B esplanade on the lagoon.

What is the price and payment plan?

Prices and the payment plan are shared by our advisor as per Sobha Realty’s current price list. Fill the form and we will send them to you.

Can Indians buy property in Dubai?

Yes. Foreign nationals, including Indian residents and NRIs, can buy freehold property in Dubai. Resident Indians usually pay through the RBI’s Liberalised Remittance Scheme — please confirm the current rules with your bank or chartered accountant.
