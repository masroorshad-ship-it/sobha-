FAQ

Can citizens of Myanmar buy?

Foreign nationals can buy freehold property in Dubai’s designated areas. Every purchase is subject to the developer’s identity and source-of-funds checks (KYC/AML); our advisor will confirm the requirements for your case.

Do I need to travel to Dubai?

You can view homes on a video call. Our advisor will explain the booking and contract steps as required by the developer.

How do I pay?

Payments are made directly to the developer’s official bank account. Please check your country’s rules on sending money abroad before you book.

When is handover?

It depends on the tower — The Horizon and The Serene: December 2029; The Mirage and The Pinnacle: December 2030 (anticipated completion).
